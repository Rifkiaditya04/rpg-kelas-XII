## Interpretation

The proposed protagonist is a Grade 12 student rather than a combat hero. The character should function as the embodied exploration and interaction layer of World 1.

The concept prioritizes:

- strong small-scale silhouette;
- school-appropriate clothing and backpack cue;
- 2D top-down / 3-quarter readability;
- simple idle/walk/interaction animations;
- touch + keyboard compatibility;
- future customization without forcing a large customization system into the first prototype.

These are proposals until the user explicitly approves them.
