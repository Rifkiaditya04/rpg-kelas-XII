**Phase:** Phase 3 — Progression / RPG Layer  
**Status:** READY FOR PROMOTION DECISION

## Evidence

World 1 Progression QA / Regression Gate passed through user-verified hosted/browser evidence. The clean-state test was performed in a private-browser context; the first successful mission immediately produced 400 XP, 1 Mission Complete, and 100% Learning Accuracy. Subsequent failure/pass attempts accumulated learning evidence without duplicating completion XP, and the state survived tab close/reopen.

## Implementation

The controlled progression prototype remains limited to the approved Progression System Specification v1. It does not introduce a universal level curve, final mastery threshold, Training Room implementation, or broader RPG economy.

## Interpretation

The Progression v1 prototype is ready to be promoted as a controlled World 1 progression layer. Promotion should preserve the current scope and then establish the next controlled design/implementation gate rather than immediately expanding into unrelated RPG systems.
