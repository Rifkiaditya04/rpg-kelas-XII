# Chapter 4: Menerapkan Kecerdasan Artifisial dalam Kehidupan Modern (Teks Eksplanasi Kompleks)
## Competency Matrix & Mission Mapping

### Scope Analysis
Chapter 4 contains 7 sub-competencies (A-G), focused on analyzing, evaluating, and writing Teks Eksplanasi Kompleks about Artificial Intelligence. 
For the Visual Novel Game, the interactive questions (Bank Soal) are best suited for Analytical tasks (A-E). Creative Writing (G) and Presenting (F) are project-based and cannot be automatically graded by a multiple-choice VN Engine.

### Mission Mapping
We will create 3 Missions for Chapter 4:
- **Misi 12 (Subbab A & B): Identifikasi dan Evaluasi Gagasan AI**
  - Focus: Identifying main ideas (Gagasan Utama vs Penjelas), types of paragraphs (Deduktif/Induktif), and evaluating argument strength.
- **Misi 13 (Subbab C & D): Struktur Teks dan Model 5R**
  - Focus: Understanding the structure of Teks Eksplanasi (Pernyataan Umum, Deretan Penjelas, Interpretasi) and applying the 5R Reflection model.
- **Misi 14 (Subbab D & E): Ciri Kebahasaan dan Keakuratan Data**
  - Focus: Identifying language features of complex explanatory texts (Passive voice, causal conjunctions, technical terms, analogies).
