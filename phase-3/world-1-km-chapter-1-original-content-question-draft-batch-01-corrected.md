### KM01-A1 — Evidence-supported action

**Activity:** A
**Teacher Guide anchor:** Aktivitas 1.1 — explicit information from a listened-to official letter
**Teacher Guide pages:** 34–35
**Cluster:** A–B — Receive & Evaluate Information
**Skill:** analyze
**Cognitive level:** analyze
**Difficulty:** medium
**Question type:** `mcq`
**Mode:** learning
**Game mechanic:** `evidence_scan`
**Tags:** `official_letter`, `relevant_information`, `evidence`

**Original scenario**

The administration of fictional SMA Cakrawala sends an official announcement to the student committee: the auditorium cannot be used from 13.00–15.00 on Tuesday because the electrical inspection team will work in the room. A committee member is preparing a rehearsal schedule.

**Teaching cue**

When evaluating an official communication, separate information that is explicitly supported by the message from assumptions that go beyond it.

**Challenge**

Which action is directly supported by the announcement?

A. Move the rehearsal planned for 13.00–15.00 to another available room.
B. Cancel every activity scheduled for Tuesday.
C. Cancel the event planned for Wednesday because the auditorium will probably remain closed.
D. Ask the inspection team to change its schedule immediately.

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The announcement only establishes that the auditorium is unavailable during 13.00–15.00 on Tuesday. Moving the rehearsal is directly supported; the other choices extend the information beyond the message.

**Student Book provenance:** Chapter 1 Activity A, pp. 5–7.
**Teacher Guide provenance:** Chapter 1 Subbab A, Aktivitas 1.1, pp. 34–35.

**Originality statement:** Scenario, names, institution, wording, options, and explanation are independently authored from the competency of evaluating information in an official communication.

### KM01-B1 — Explicit vs implicit meaning

**Activity:** B
**Teacher Guide anchor:** Aktivitas 1.4 — comparing explicit and implicit meanings of two official letters
**Teacher Guide pages:** 38–42
**Cluster:** A–B — Receive & Evaluate Information
**Skill:** analyze
**Cognitive level:** analyze
**Difficulty:** medium
**Question type:** `mcq`
**Mode:** learning
**Game mechanic:** `meaning_check`
**Tags:** `official_letter`, `explicit`, `implicit`

**Original paired communications**

**Letter 1:** A community learning center asks registered participants to confirm attendance by Thursday at 16.00. Confirmation can be sent through the registration desk or the center's official email.

**Letter 2:** The center announces that participants who do not confirm by the stated deadline will not be included in the final seating list.

**Challenge**

Which statement is an inference rather than a message stated directly in either letter?

A. Participants have a Thursday 16.00 confirmation deadline.
B. Confirmation can be sent through the registration desk.
C. A participant who misses the deadline may have difficulty securing a seat in the planned arrangement.
D. The second letter discusses the seating list.

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A, B, and D are directly established by the communications. C is a reasonable inference from the consequence described in the second letter, but its wording is not stated directly.

**Student Book provenance:** Chapter 1 Activity B, pp. 8–11.
**Teacher Guide provenance:** Chapter 1 Subbab B, Aktivitas 1.4, pp. 38–42.

**Originality statement:** Both communications and all answer choices are newly authored.

### KM01-B2 — Evidence-based comparison

**Activity:** B
**Teacher Guide anchor:** Aktivitas 1.4 — comparing/evaluating explicit and implicit meanings of two official letters
**Teacher Guide pages:** 38–42
**Cluster:** A–B — Receive & Evaluate Information
**Skill:** evaluate
**Cognitive level:** evaluate
**Difficulty:** hard
**Question type:** `mcq`
**Mode:** learning
**Game mechanic:** `comparison_scan`
**Tags:** `official_letter`, `comparison`, `evidence`

**Original paired communications**

**Letter 1:** The school laboratory will be unavailable on Friday morning for equipment inspection. Students may submit completed laboratory reports through the collection box outside the laboratory.

**Letter 2:** The school library will be unavailable on Friday morning for inventory. Students may renew borrowed books through the library's online service.

**Challenge**

Which comparison is best supported?

A. Both facilities remain physically open because an alternative service is available.
B. Both facilities are unavailable on Friday morning, but each provides a different alternative for a related student need.
C. Both facilities use an online service for every student request.
D. The laboratory accepts online reports during the closure.

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Both letters establish a Friday-morning physical closure. The laboratory provides a physical collection box, while the library provides an online renewal service. The alternatives and purposes differ.

**Student Book provenance:** Chapter 1 Activity B, pp. 8–11.
**Teacher Guide provenance:** Chapter 1 Subbab B, Aktivitas 1.4, pp. 38–42.

**Originality statement:** Both official communications are fictional and independently authored.

### KM01-C1 — Reasoned reflection

**Activity:** C
**Teacher Guide anchor:** Aktivitas 1.5 — reflecting on the writer's ideas in an official letter
**Teacher Guide pages:** 43–45
**Cluster:** C — Reflect & Interpret
**Skill:** apply
**Cognitive level:** apply
**Difficulty:** medium
**Question type:** `mcq`
**Mode:** learning
**Game mechanic:** `scenario_decision`
**Tags:** `official_letter`, `reflection`, `context`

**Original scenario**

A fictional school sends an official letter informing student organizations that requests to borrow school equipment must be submitted at least two school days before the activity so the equipment team can check availability and condition.

The science club plans to borrow a projector tomorrow but has not submitted a request.

**Challenge**

Which response best reflects the information in the official letter?

A. Submit the request according to the stated procedure and ask what alternative is available if the two-day requirement cannot be met.
B. Take a projector from the equipment room because the activity is educational.
C. Ask another organization to submit the request using its own activity details.
D. Assume the two-day rule does not apply to science activities.

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A respects the stated procedure while addressing the practical situation. The other responses invent an exception or bypass the official process.

**Student Book provenance:** Chapter 1 Activity C, pp. 12–13.
**Teacher Guide provenance:** Chapter 1 Subbab C, Aktivitas 1.5, pp. 43–45.

**Scope note:** This is a constrained preparatory micro-challenge, not a complete replacement for the source reflection activity. The Teacher Guide's full reflection criteria include identifying important ideas, strengths/weaknesses with reasons, connection to personal experience, change in viewpoint, and a real-life application. A follow-up reflection step should be added if this competency is promoted beyond micro-challenge status.

**Originality statement:** Scenario and response options are independently authored.

**Tags:** `official_letter`, `structure`, `function`

**Original document fragment**

A fictional school prepares an official invitation. Before the opening greeting, the document contains the name and complete address of the organization that is expected to receive the letter.

**Challenge**

What is the primary function of this part of the official letter?

A. Identify the intended recipient and address of the communication.
B. Explain the sender's personal reason for writing.
C. Present the main activity schedule.
D. State the sender's closing hope.

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The recipient/address component establishes who the communication is directed to and where it is addressed. The other functions belong to other parts of the letter.

### KM01-D2 — Language-use diagnosis

**Activity:** D
**Teacher Guide anchor:** Aktivitas 1.7 — analyzing language use in an official circular letter
**Teacher Guide pages:** 46–49
**Cluster:** D–E — Inspect Official Communication
**Skill:** evaluate
**Cognitive level:** evaluate
**Difficulty:** medium
**Question type:** `mcq`
**Mode:** learning
**Game mechanic:** `language_scan`
**Tags:** `official_letter`, `standard_words`, `effective_sentence`

**Original draft sentence**

“Untuk informasi lebih lanjut, peserta bisa hubungi panitia secepatnya ya.”

**Challenge**

Which revision is most appropriate for an official letter?

A. “Untuk informasi lebih lanjut, peserta dapat menghubungi panitia sesegera mungkin.”
B. “Kalau ada info, peserta bisa hubungi panitia secepatnya, ya.”
C. “Peserta wajib banget hubungi panitia kalau ada pertanyaan.”
D. “Untuk informasi lebih lanjut, peserta hubungi panitia aja.”

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A uses more formal and effective wording while preserving the intended meaning. B, C, and D retain conversational or unnecessarily informal expressions.

**Teacher Guide provenance:** Chapter 1 Subbab D, Aktivitas 1.7, pp. 46–49.
