# World 1 — Kurikulum Merdeka Standard Chapter 1 Content Rebaseline v1

**Status:** PROPOSAL — AWAITING USER APPROVAL
**Phase:** Phase 3 — Content / Gameplay Rebaseline
**World:** World 1

## 1. Purpose

Re-establish the academic source and gameplay domain for future World 1 content using the current standard Kurikulum Merdeka Bahasa Indonesia Class XII source set already stored in the repository.

This proposal does **not** delete or rewrite previously approved prototype datasets. Those remain available for regression/history. It establishes the source basis for future new main-game content.

`Buku Kurikulum/Kurikulum Merdeka/Indonesia_BS_KLS_XII_Rev_.md`

Revised 2025 edition, Bahasa Indonesia untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XII.

### Teacher Guide

The Student Book states that it contains five chapters. Chapter 1 is:

**Mengomunikasikan Informasi Penting tentang Pendidikan dan/atau Pekerjaan**.

## 3. Chapter 1 source scope

The Student Book table of contents identifies these Chapter 1 activities:

- A — Mengevaluasi Isi Rekaman Surat Resmi yang Disimak
- B — Mengevaluasi Pesan Tersurat dan Tersirat dari Dua Surat Resmi yang Berbeda
- C — Merefleksikan Gagasan Berdasarkan Isi Surat Resmi
- D — Menganalisis Struktur dan Penggunaan Bahasa pada Surat Resmi
- E — Mempresentasikan Kesalahan Kaidah Penulisan Contoh Surat Resmi
- F — Menulis Surat Resmi dengan Memperhatikan Penggunaan Kata Baku dan Kalimat Efektif
- G — Membalas Pesan Surat Resmi yang Diterima Menggunakan Layanan Pos Elektronik
- H — Memublikasikan Poster Pengumuman Berdasarkan Surat Edaran yang Diperoleh

The exact learning interpretation and skill mapping must be validated against the corresponding Student Book sections and Teacher Guide before question drafting.

## 4. Proposed World 1 gameplay interpretation

The academic domain changes from the older prototype's job-application/CV focus to the current standard Kurikulum Merdeka Chapter 1 focus on communicating important information about education/work through official letters and related communication tasks.

Possible gameplay translation — **proposal only**:

```text
Explore School / Career Communication Area
        ↓
NPC / Teacher Context
        ↓
Learn: Why official communication matters
        ↓
Mission A — Listen / evaluate official-letter information
        ↓
Mission B — Detect explicit / implicit messages
        ↓
Mission C — Reflect / connect ideas
        ↓
Mission D — Inspect structure + language
        ↓
Mission E — Present / repair writing conventions
        ↓
Mission F — Compose an official letter
        ↓
Mission G — Reply through electronic mail
        ↓
Mission H — Publish an announcement poster
```

This does **not** mean every subsection must become one mission. Mission boundaries should follow narrative coherence and learning coverage.

## 5. Content-to-game principle

The game remains a Light RPG Educational Platform.

The transformation should be:

```text
Competency / learning goal
        ↓
Contextual problem
        ↓
NPC / environment / evidence
        ↓
Learn / explanation / guided example
        ↓
Challenge
        ↓
Feedback
        ↓
Progression
```

The project must not simply replace the old questions with a new list of quiz questions.

## 6. Question-count policy

There is no fixed number of questions per chapter or subsection.

The required challenge count will be determined from:

- narrative coverage;
- learning-goal coverage;
- competency/skill coverage;
- cognitive demand;
- interaction diversity;
- redundancy removal;
- QA capacity.

A broad Chapter 1 may require substantially more challenges than a narrow section.

## 7. Copyright-safe authoring

The source books provide the academic basis, not text to copy into the game.

Production content should avoid:
- verbatim textbook questions;
- copied passages used as dialogue without rights clearance;
- copied answer choices/distractors;
- trivial paraphrase intended to disguise copying;
- copied figures/tables/illustrations without appropriate rights.

Prefer:
- independently authored explanations;
- independently authored scenarios;
- original question wording;
- original distractors;
- original examples;
- licensed or independently created visual assets.

Provenance must still record the academic source concept/section.

## 8. Legacy content boundary

Existing World 1 approved A–C and D–F pools remain additive/versioned artifacts and must not be deleted merely because this rebaseline changes the future source policy.

However, new main-game content should **not** continue extending the older job-application/CV source basis until this rebaseline is approved and the new source mapping is established.

The existing playable Career Mission may remain available for regression and architecture testing during the transition.

## 9. TKA boundary

K13 remains relevant to the future TKA Preparation / Academic Planning layer according to the project's TKA evidence and assessment-framework intersection.

It is not a reason to use K13 as the source for new main-game World 1 content under the current project direction.
