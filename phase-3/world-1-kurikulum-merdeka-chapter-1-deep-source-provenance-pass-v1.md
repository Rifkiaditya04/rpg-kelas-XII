## 3. Chapter 1 authoritative scope
Student Book Chapter 1 title:

**Mengomunikasikan Informasi Penting tentang Pendidikan dan/atau Pekerjaan**

The Student Book table of contents identifies eight learning activities:

A. Mengevaluasi Isi Rekaman Surat Resmi yang Disimak
B. Mengevaluasi Pesan Tersurat dan Tersirat dari Dua Surat Resmi yang Berbeda
C. Merefleksikan Gagasan Berdasarkan Isi Surat Resmi
D. Menganalisis Struktur dan Penggunaan Bahasa pada Surat Resmi
E. Mempresentasikan Kesalahan Kaidah Penulisan Contoh Surat Resmi
F. Menulis Surat Resmi dengan Memperhatikan Penggunaan Kata Baku dan Kalimat Efektif
G. Membalas Pesan Surat Resmi yang Diterima Menggunakan Layanan Pos Elektronik
H. Memublikasikan Poster Pengumuman Berdasarkan Surat Edaran yang Diperoleh

The Student Book TOC places Chapter 1 at pages 1 onward and the next chapter at page 49.

## 8. Copyright-safe authoring boundary
The source books are copyrighted educational works. For this project:

### Allowed basis
- identify concepts, skills, learning objectives, text genres, and pedagogical intent;
- use source page/section as provenance;
- write new explanations in original wording;
- create original scenarios and examples that test the same source-grounded competency;
- create original distractors and answer choices;
- cite the source internally in provenance metadata.

### Do not copy
- textbook paragraphs verbatim;
- textbook questions verbatim;
- textbook exercises with only superficial wording changes;
- distinctive examples or passages as game content;
- answer keys copied from the textbook as if they were original game content;
- textbook illustrations or other protected visual assets unless separately licensed.

The game should teach the same source-grounded competencies through original content, not reproduce the book.
