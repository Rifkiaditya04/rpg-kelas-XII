# Chapter 3: Menciptakan Lingkungan Sekolah Aman dan Nyaman (Novel)
## Competency Matrix & Mission Mapping

### Scope Analysis
Chapter 3 contains 7 sub-competencies (A-G), focused on Literary Analysis (Novel) and Creative Writing.
For the Visual Novel Game, the interactive questions (Bank Soal) are best suited for Analytical tasks (A-D) and Language Mechanics (F). Creative Writing (E, G) are project-based and cannot be automatically graded by a multiple-choice VN Engine.

### Mission Mapping
We will create 3 Missions for Chapter 3:
- **Misi 9 (Subbab A & B): Sudut Pandang dan Perwatakan**
  - Focus: Identifying POV (Orang Pertama, Orang Ketiga, dsb.) and analyzing character traits from Laskar Pelangi excerpts.
- **Misi 10 (Subbab C & D): Alur Cerita dan Pesan Moral**
  - Focus: Evaluating plot development and concluding the moral message.
- **Misi 11 (Subbab F): Kaidah Bahasa & Tanda Baca Sastra**
  - Focus: Using correct punctuation and descriptive language in story writing.
